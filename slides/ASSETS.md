# Slide assets

Files under `public/assets/` used by `slides.md`, with their sources. Crops and
generated plots were made with standalone scripts, not the notebooks.

## `geometry_bkg8/` (bad geometry)

The `zoom_*.png` originals were generated by `--dump-zoom-geometry` with
`ZoomRegion:bkg_top_level_cells:8` and plotted with
`tools/zooms/plot_zoom_geometry.py`. They were supplied directly and are kept as
sources; only the crops are used in slides.

| File | Source | Used on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `cell_types.png` | `zoom_cell_types.png`, legend cropped off (redrawn in HTML) | Bad geometry, bad vs good |
| `occupancy_dm.png` | Left (DM) panel of `zoom_occupancy_histograms.png` | Occupancy |
| `zoom_cell_types.png`, `zoom_occupancy_histograms.png`, `zoom_padding_analysis.png`, `zoom_radial_distributions.png` | Originals | Not used |
